RadioLand Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

RadioLand is a hobby project built and maintained by Nick Langan (W2NJL). This policy explains what the RadioLand mobile app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data and we don't run advertising.

1. Information the App Collects

Location

With your permission, RadioLand reads your device's current location (latitude and longitude) so it can show radio stations receivable in your area, draw signal-strength predictions from your position, and compute distance and bearing to transmitters.

  • You can grant or deny location access at any time through your device's system settings. The app also lets you pick a location manually instead of using GPS.
  • Coordinates are sent to the RadioLand server for the duration of a query. We do not create a profile linking your location history to an identity.
  • We do not track your location in the background. Location is read only while the app is open.

Crash and Error Reports

To diagnose bugs, RadioLand uses Sentry to transmit automated crash reports. A report typically includes device model, operating system version, app version, the code path that crashed, and non-personal technical context. Reports are retained by Sentry under their published privacy policy and are used solely to fix bugs in future releases.

Device Preferences

Your in-app settings — such as broadcast band (FM/AM/weather), sort order, measurement units, filter choices, and "first launch" state — are stored locally on your device using Flutter's shared_preferences. These values stay on your device and are not transmitted.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not request or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • RadioLand has no user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
  • There is no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no Firebase Analytics), and no behavioral tracking.
  • Microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, camera and SMS access are never requested.

2. How Your Information Is Used

The data described above is used only to deliver the app's core functionality:

  • Location is used to return a list of nearby radio stations from the FCC-derived database and to power features like coverage maps, interference analysis, and sports-affiliate lookups.
  • Crash reports are used to identify and fix defects.
  • Local preferences are used to remember how you like the app configured between sessions.

We do not sell, rent, or share any of this information with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

Some features connect to third-party services that have their own privacy practices:

  • Sentry — anonymized crash and error reports. Sentry privacy policy
  • OpenStreetMap & MapTiler — background map tiles. Your device's IP address is visible to these services when map tiles are fetched. OSM copyright · MaptTiler privacy
  • Google Fonts — font files served by Google when the app launches.
  • External links — tapping a link to a third-party site (e.g. christmas.asherr.org, the FCC Public Files, the RadioLand blog, Ko-fi, a station's website) opens that site in your system browser and is subject to that site's privacy practices.
  • Email composition — using the in-app feedback option opens your device's mail client with a draft message. No email is sent unless you choose to send it.

4. Children's Privacy

RadioLand is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

5. Data Retention

Location coordinates are used in the moment of a query and are not retained in a persistent user-linked log on the server. Crash reports are retained by Sentry for the duration described in their policy. Local preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

6. Your Choices

  • Revoke location access at any time via your device's system settings. The app will continue to function; you'll be prompted to enter a location manually.
  • Reset your preferences by uninstalling and reinstalling the app.
  • Delete data — because RadioLand does not maintain a personal account or server-side profile, there is no account to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

7. Security

Network requests from the app to the RadioLand server use HTTPS where configured. Sentry transmits crash reports over HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable technical measures to protect the limited data the app handles.

8. International Users

RadioLand is operated from the United States. If you use the app from outside the U.S., the limited data described above may be transmitted to and processed on servers located in the U.S.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Effective" date above will change and the updated version will be posted at https://w2njl.github.io/radioland/privacy.html. Material changes may also be announced in the app or on the RadioLand blog.
